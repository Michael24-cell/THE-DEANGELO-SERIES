The Metamorphosis

Oil on linen — MMXXIV

"A butterfly built from what it used to be — half-skull, half-wing, suspended at the moment of changing its mind."

The Metamorphosis began as a small graphite study, sketched in the studio over the winter of 2023. The final canvas was completed nine months later, oil on Belgian linen, after the motif had moved through three earlier drafts on paper.

The piece was reproduced for Series 01 as a six-colour water-based print, hand-pulled in Brooklyn on heavyweight cotton. The print is the painting's only authorised wearable edition.

By Marco Deangelo · Brooklyn studio · MMXXIV
