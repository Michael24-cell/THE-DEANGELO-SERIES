The Statement

The DeAngelo Series begins with a painting.

Not a licensed graphic. Not a manufactured pattern. A painting — Venezia, 1979, watercolor. Isola di San Giorgio Maggiore, Venice. Translated once onto heavyweight cotton through a process that refuses speed.

Each piece is produced after an order is placed. Gallery-grade water-based screen printing. Hand-pulled.

When a series closes, its pieces move to The Archive and the work is documented.

The series is not fashion. It is collected.
