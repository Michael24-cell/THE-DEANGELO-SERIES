Villa d'Este Tivoli, Italy  ·  1980

Tony DeAngelo

Tony is an award-winning architect, developer, business leader, designer, writer, and artist. His love of all creative things, both old and new, reflects in this series of vintage photographs and art in all media.

His love of travel and history are exemplified in the bespoke pieces selected for our curation of elegant t-shirts and crews. You will find in the collection an affinity for European history and a tribute to his life living on both coasts of America.
