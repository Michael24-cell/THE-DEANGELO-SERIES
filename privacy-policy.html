Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected and used when you visit our website, place an order, or interact with our products and services. This website is operated by Tony DeAngelo, doing business as The DeAngelo Series (“we,” “us,” or “our”).

By using our website, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information needed to operate our website, process orders, fulfill purchases, provide customer support, and improve our services.

Information You Provide

When you place an order, contact us, or interact with our website, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number, if provided
  • Billing address
  • Shipping address
  • Order details, including product, size, color, quantity, and purchase history
  • Customer support messages or other communications you send to us

Payment Information

Payments are processed through third-party payment providers, including Stripe.

We do not store full credit card numbers on our own servers. Stripe may collect and process payment details, billing information, transaction information, device information, and fraud prevention data according to its own privacy and security practices.

We may receive limited payment-related information from Stripe, such as payment status, transaction ID, billing details, card brand, and the last four digits of a payment card.

Fulfillment and Shipping Information

We use third-party fulfillment partners, including Printify and its print providers, to produce and ship made-to-order products.

To fulfill your order, we may share necessary order information with these partners, including your name, shipping address, product selection, size, color, quantity, and shipping details.

Customer Accounts and Order Records

We do not currently offer customer accounts or maintain a separate customer account database on our own website.

However, personal information connected to orders, payments, fulfillment, shipping, customer support, and business records may be stored or processed through third-party services we use, including Stripe, Printify, website hosting providers, email providers, shipping carriers, and fulfillment partners.

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, certain information may be collected automatically by our website, hosting provider, analytics tools, or similar technologies. This may include:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Pages viewed
  • Referring website or source
  • Date and time of visit
  • General website usage data
  • Server log information

We use this information to help operate the website, monitor performance, prevent fraud or misuse, troubleshoot errors, and improve the customer experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process and complete orders
  • Accept and verify payments
  • Produce, fulfill, and ship products
  • Send order confirmations, shipping updates, and customer service messages
  • Respond to questions, requests, or support inquiries
  • Prevent fraud, unauthorized transactions, chargebacks, and misuse of our website
  • Improve our website, products, and customer experience
  • Maintain business, tax, accounting, and legal records
  • Comply with applicable laws and regulations
  • Send marketing emails or promotional messages only where permitted and, when required, with your consent

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information for money.

We may share information with trusted third parties when necessary to operate our business, process orders, fulfill purchases, provide customer support, protect our website, or comply with legal obligations.

We may share information with:

  • Payment processors, including Stripe
  • Fulfillment and print-on-demand partners, including Printify and its print providers
  • Shipping carriers
  • Website hosting providers
  • Email and customer support providers
  • Analytics providers, if used
  • Marketing tools, if used
  • Fraud prevention and security providers
  • Legal, tax, accounting, or business advisors
  • Government authorities or law enforcement when required by law

We only share information as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website may use cookies, pixels, or similar technologies to help the site function, improve performance, understand visitor behavior, and enhance the shopping experience.

Cookies and similar technologies may be used to:

  • Keep the website functioning properly
  • Remember preferences
  • Analyze traffic and website performance
  • Understand how customers interact with our site
  • Support marketing or advertising tools, if enabled

You may be able to control or disable cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling cookies may affect how certain parts of the website function.

If we begin using specific analytics or advertising tools, such as Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, TikTok Pixel, or email marketing platforms, we may update this Privacy Policy to identify those providers and explain how they are used.

5. Third-Party Services

Our website and business operations rely on third-party services. These third parties may collect, process, or store information according to their own privacy policies.

Examples include:

  • Stripe for payment processing
  • Printify and print providers for product fulfillment
  • Shipping carriers for delivery
  • Website hosting providers
  • Email and customer support providers
  • Analytics or marketing platforms, if used

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites, platforms, or services that we do not own or control.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • Process and fulfill orders
  • Provide customer support
  • Maintain business records
  • Prevent fraud and resolve disputes
  • Comply with legal, tax, accounting, and regulatory obligations

Because we use third-party providers such as Stripe and Printify, some order, payment, and fulfillment information may also be retained by those providers according to their own policies.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to help protect personal information.

Payment information is processed through third-party payment providers such as Stripe. We do not store full credit card numbers on our own servers.

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of any information transmitted through or stored by our website or third-party providers.

8. Your Privacy Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information. These rights may include the ability to request:

  • Access to personal information we have about you
  • Correction of inaccurate information
  • Deletion of certain personal information
  • Restriction or objection to certain processing
  • Information about how your data is used or shared

To make a privacy request, contact us at support@thedeangeloseries.com.

We may need to verify your identity before completing certain requests. Some information may be retained where required or permitted by law, such as for tax, accounting, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legal compliance purposes.

9. California Privacy Notice

If you are a California resident, you may have additional privacy rights under California law.

We may collect the following categories of personal information:

  • Identifiers, such as name, email address, billing address, shipping address, and IP address
  • Commercial information, such as products purchased, order details, and purchase history
  • Internet or electronic activity information, such as website usage data and server logs
  • Payment-related information processed through third-party payment providers
  • Communications you send to us

We use this information for the business purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including order processing, payment processing, fulfillment, shipping, customer support, fraud prevention, website operation, marketing if you opt in, and legal compliance.

We do not sell your personal information for money. We also do not currently share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising unless specifically disclosed in this Privacy Policy.

If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at support@thedeangeloseries.com, and we will take appropriate steps to delete the information where required.

You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time by using the unsubscribe link in the email or by contacting us at support@thedeangeloseries.com.

Even if you unsubscribe from marketing messages, we may still send non-promotional messages related to orders, shipping, customer service, refunds, policy updates, or other business purposes.

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or policies of third-party websites.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party websites you visit.

Your continued use of our website after changes are posted means you acknowledge the updated Privacy Policy.
